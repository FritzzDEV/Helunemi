Privacy notice

Helunemi is operated by Fritzz Xenon, its owner. We use account information to provide profiles, authentication, community features, purchases when available, and protection against misuse.

Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, this includes your username, account ID, authentication email, verification state, password hash for site authentication, optional contact details and country, profile images and text, roles, favorites, Friend-Cards, uploads, posts, friend relationships, chat messages, reports, and ticket inventory. Passwords are not displayed or stored as readable profile fields. Firebase manages email authentication when configured; existing site accounts also use salted password hashes.

Requests necessarily expose connection information to the server and hosting provider. Temporary rate-limit counters use a network address and, for sign-in protection, an account identifier. Errors may be recorded in server logs to diagnose failures. Do not put passwords, private keys, or sensitive personal information into reports or public posts.

What others can see

Your public profile and posted Friend-Card can be viewed by other visitors. Posts and uploads follow their selected audience where supported. Account email and personal contact fields are not part of the public profile response. Chat messages are available to the participants; reports go to the owner and authorized studio staff. Messages and reports are stored on the service and are not end-to-end encrypted.

Storage & service providers

Render hosts the site. Depending on the deployment settings, Firebase handles authentication and legacy media, and configured database and file-storage providers store account data and uploads. These providers process the data needed for their functions and may store it outside your country. External links have separate privacy practices; remote media and Google Fonts can receive your connection information when loaded.

We do not use this notice as permission for advertising tracking. Clearing browser data removes local settings but does not delete your server account.

Your choices & account deletion

Use Profile to review and edit your information, choose what you publish, or use the Danger zone to pause or delete your account. Report an issue. Protect a creator.

Send a report through Community → Report a bug or issue. You can follow replies in My reports. This channel also accepts privacy requests, copying complaints, and security concerns. You need an account to use the on-site report inbox.

If you cannot sign in, join the official Discord community and privately contact Fritzz Xenon. Discord is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Content or copying complaint: include the original work, the page or copied location, your relationship to the rights holder, and the action requested. Do not publish private documents in a public channel.

We aim to review reports as a small team, but cannot promise a fixed response time or a reward. Helunemi's safeguards reduce risk; they do not guarantee uninterrupted service or absolute security. Nothing here limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be limited.
